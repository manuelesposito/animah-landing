What is Animah?

Animah listens to you. Over time, it builds a private model of your thoughts, memories, and patterns — then speaks back as a version of you that has lived through everything you have.

Not a chatbot. Not a journal. Something closer to a conversation with the person you are becoming.

Your voice stays on your device. Your memories stay yours. There is no server learning from you, no company storing your innermost thoughts. Just you, talking to yourself — across time.

Built on three principles

I.

Local first

Everything stays on your device. Your memories never leave. No exceptions.

II.

Open source

Inspect it, fork it, trust it. The code is public because your privacy shouldn't depend on blind faith.

III.

Zero cloud

No accounts. No sync servers. No data collection. Nothing that could be breached, sold, or subpoenaed.
